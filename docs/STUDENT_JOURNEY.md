# What a student experiences

The course is eight stages. An average student who does the homework can finish. The site does not open the next lesson early, and it does not graduate anyone by itself.

Times on deadlines are shown twice: Europe/Amsterdam, and the clock on the student's own device.

## Before week 1

The student receives one invite link. They enter a display name, an email, and a password of at least 12 characters, and they accept a short notice that work is stored on the academy server. No email is sent back. If they lose the password, the login page says: "Forgot your password? Ask your mentor for a reset link." The mentor runs `sfa reset-password` and shares a one-time link that lasts 30 minutes. Using that link signs them out of every device.

Telegram is optional. The course works without it.

## Every week, the same loop

The dashboard's first block is "What to do next".

1. **Lesson open.** Read the current lesson (M1–M5, then A6–A8) and submit the full quiz. A refresh keeps the answers on that device until submit. The quiz does not open the next lesson.
2. **Waiting.** Echo reviews the queue. There is no guaranteed hour. The student should not resubmit and should not try the next lesson by typing its address. A locked lesson shows the title only.
3. **Assignment.** At 80% the quiz is approved. From 60% to 79% it is partial: the student still gets an assignment and corrections, and still does not skip ahead. Below 60% they restudy and retake. The assignment includes drills for open weaknesses and, from week 2 on, a spiral review of every earlier stage. The deadline is about a week out.
4. **Upload.** Up to 12 files, 20 MB each: PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD, PNG, JPG. HEIC phone photos must be exported to JPEG. Persian filenames are fine. Empty files are rejected. Screenshots alone are rejected. Upload, then press final submit. Pressing twice does not create a second submission.
5. **Practical result.** 80 or above is approved. 65 to 79 is partial. Both can open the next lesson. Below 65, or any hard fail, starts a remedial week at the same stage. Hard fails: a mental stop instead of a structural hard stop, risk above 1% of equity, or net reward below 1:2.

## Week by week

The lessons stay locked until the previous practical is positive. Typing `/academy/missions/M5` in week 1 shows a lock, not the quiz and not the answer key.

| Week | Lesson | What "done" means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | M1 | Quiz reviewed, assignment uploaded, practical positive. Beginners often miss items and still pass as partial if they are at 60% or above. |
| 2 | M2 | Same loop, plus a spiral task from week 1. |
| 3 | M3 | Same loop, spiral from weeks 1 and 2. |
| 4 | M4 | Same loop. |
| 5 | M5 | Same loop. This is the end of the first five missions. |
| 6 | A6 | Advanced module. Still the same quiz-then-practical gate. |
| 7 | A7 | Same gate. |
| 8 | A8 | A positive practical does not open a ninth stage. The dashboard says the eight weeks are complete and the senior mentor session is next. |

A failed practical does not delete the week. It adds a remedial week at that same stage, with a new assignment. The student can resubmit. Two clean weeks of evidence are required before a recorded weakness is marked resolved.

## What the student never gets

- The answer key, another student's files, or another student's grades.
- A lesson opened by a bookmark or by typing a later week's address. The header no longer shows a local XP score.
- An email that says the password was reset or the quiz was graded.
- A diploma from the software. Week 8 ends with a person.
